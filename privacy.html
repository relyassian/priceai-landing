Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 7, 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains exactly what data Price AI collects, why we collect it, and how we protect it. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

1. About Price AI

Price AI ("we," "our," or "us") is a mobile application developed and operated by Price AI Inc. The app uses artificial intelligence and camera access to identify products and provide real-time price comparisons across 50+ retailers. This Privacy Policy applies to the Price AI mobile application and the website trypriceai.app.

2. Information We Collect

Account information: When you create an account, we collect your email address and name.

Camera and images: When you scan a product, the image is sent to our AI provider for product identification. Images are stored in our secure cloud storage to display your scan history.

Scan history: We store the products you've scanned, the prices retrieved, and your Deal Scores so you can access them later.

Usage data: We collect information about how you use the app — screens viewed, features used, and actions taken — to improve the product.

Device information: We may collect device type, operating system version, and app version for debugging and performance monitoring.

Attribution data and advertising identifier: If you downloaded the app through a marketing campaign, we collect attribution data — including your device's Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) on iOS, where permitted — to understand which channels are effective. IDFA collection requires your permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt.

Onboarding survey responses: During onboarding, we collect your answers to preference questions — including your shopping goal, shopping frequency, preferred shopping locations, product categories of interest, biggest shopping frustration, savings goals, and how you heard about us. This data is used solely to personalize your in-app experience and is never sold or shared with third parties.

Push notification token: If you grant permission, we collect your device's push notification token to send you re-engagement reminders and relevant alerts. You can revoke this permission at any time in your device's notification settings.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the Price AI service
  • To identify products from your camera scans
  • To display price comparisons and Deal Scores
  • To store your scan history and saved items
  • To send transactional emails (account verification, password reset)
  • To analyze app usage and fix bugs
  • To measure marketing effectiveness
  • To personalize your app experience based on your onboarding survey responses
  • To send push notifications, including re-engagement reminders (only with your permission)

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate Price AI:

  • Supabase — database and authentication. Your account data and scan history are stored securely on Supabase servers.
  • OpenAI — AI product identification. Images you scan are sent to OpenAI's API for processing. OpenAI's privacy policy applies to this data.
  • RevenueCat — subscription management. Handles your subscription status and billing through Apple's App Store.
  • PostHog — product analytics. We use PostHog to understand how users interact with the app. Data is anonymized where possible.
  • AppsFlyer — marketing attribution. Helps us understand how you discovered Price AI.
  • Apple Push Notification service (APNs) — push notification delivery. Your device's push notification token is transmitted to APNs to deliver re-engagement reminders. Apple's privacy policy governs APNs data handling.
  • SerpAPI / Bing — price data retrieval. Product searches are sent to these services to retrieve current prices.

5. Affiliate Disclosure

Price AI participates in affiliate programs, including the Amazon Associates Program and eBay Partner Network. When you click a retailer link in the app and make a purchase, we may earn a small commission at no additional cost to you. This does not affect the prices shown or our Deal Score calculations.

6. Data Retention

We retain your account data and scan history for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at hello@trypriceai.app. We will process deletion requests within 30 days.

7. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures including row-level security on our database, encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), and secure cloud storage. However, no system is completely secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

Price AI is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights including access to your data, correction of inaccurate data, deletion of your data, and opting out of certain data processing. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@trypriceai.app.

10. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

California residents have the right to know what personal information we collect, request deletion of their data, and opt out of the sale of personal information. We do not sell personal information. To submit a request, email hello@trypriceai.app.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at hello@trypriceai.app.
