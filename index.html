FAQ

Questions,
answered

Everything you need to know before you scan.

Which retailers does Price AI compare?
Price AI compares prices across 50+ retailers — including names like Nike, Best Buy, Walmart, Target, and eBay — depending on what you scan. Retailer coverage varies by product category and is always expanding.
Does it work on used or secondhand items?
Price AI is built to identify products and match them against current retail listings, so it works best on items still sold new. It can surface resale listings — like on eBay — when relevant, but Deal Scores are most reliable for new, in-production items where condition doesn't affect the price.
What if Price AI can't find enough prices?
If we can't gather enough matching price data for a confident comparison, Price AI shows you whatever retailer results it did find rather than guess at a Deal Score it can't back up.
What happens after my free trial ends?
Your 3-day free trial gives you full access to Price AI's price comparison and Deal Score features. If you don't cancel at least 24 hours before it ends, you'll be charged for the plan you chose — $9.99/month or $39.99/year — and your subscription will auto-renew until you cancel.
How do I cancel my subscription?
Cancel anytime in your Apple ID Account Settings. Canceling stops future billing — you'll keep access until the end of your current billing period, and no refunds are given for partial periods.
Are my photos stored?
Yes. When you scan an item, that photo is sent to our AI provider to identify the product and stored in secure cloud storage so it shows up in your scan history. You can delete your account and all associated data anytime by emailing hello@trypriceai.app — see our Privacy Policy for details.
Does Price AI work outside the US?
Price AI is built around US retailers and pricing in US dollars, so it's optimized for shopping in the United States. You can download and use the app elsewhere, but retailer coverage and price accuracy outside the US may be limited.
